The Gauntlet of the Red Waste

Twenty clicks to Bleak Flats, and Jacob has driven every one of them more times than he’d like. The road is not the only thing out here that moves.

At the fork, take the Old Canyon Road through the gums and the spinifex, or run straight across the salt pans where the striders hunt. Climb down to look round ruins, an old strip mine and three shallow caves, each with something left in it. Learn what lives here first: everything out here wants something different from you.

Driving
W/S throttle and brake, A/D steer, mouse looks around, F drink from the canister, G Sulphur-Salts into the intake, R the radio, V seat or chase view, E climb down.
On foot
WASD walk, Shift run, E use or climb aboard, F drink from the water bag. Walk into a cave mouth to go in, and back out into the light to leave.
Always
C compass, Tab the notebook, N jot an idea, Esc pause.

What the books and the Field Guide say is tagged Canon: the route, the gums, the spinifex, the striders, the swarm at the wreck, the gate. The caves, the ruins and the relics are Experimental. Turn on Workshop view to see which is which.
